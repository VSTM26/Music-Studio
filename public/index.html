How It Works

Simple enough for normal users

01

Download the app

Download the actual desktop app bundle for Windows, macOS, or Linux and unpack it on the user’s machine.

02

Open the app

Launch the packaged GUI app directly instead of dealing with Python, terminals, or helper scripts.

03

Choose a folder and download

Then sign into YouTube in the normal browser, import the session, and save files into the chosen folder.
