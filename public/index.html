How It Works

Three simple steps

  1. 01

    Open YouTube in your browser

    Use a normal browser tab on the user's own computer. Sign in there with the user's own YouTube account.

  2. 02

    Choose a local folder

    In Chrome or Edge, pick the folder on your own machine where finished files should land.

  3. 03

    Start the download

    Paste the links you want. When the job finishes, Music Studio saves the files into the folder you picked.
