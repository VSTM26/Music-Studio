YouTube Music Export Workspace

Liked Music Studio

Launch a guided Chrome session, sign in to YouTube Music, and export your liked songs into shareable .txt, .csv, and .json files. Then optionally download all or only the tracks you select with yt-dlp, with optional MP3 extraction through ffmpeg. The guided browser uses its own local profile, so any user can log in with their own YouTube Music account.

Workflow

Three clean steps

  1. 01

    Open the guided browser

    We use a dedicated Chrome profile so the export can access a stable debug session.

  2. 02

    Sign in to YouTube Music

    Sign in with your own account in the special Chrome window, then keep it open while exporting.

  3. 03

    Run the export

    Results land in your selected save folder and are previewed below. Reset the guided session any time you want to switch accounts.
