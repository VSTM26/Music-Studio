How It Works

Three simple steps

  1. 01

    Connect YouTube

    Open YouTube in your normal browser, sign in there, then import that session into Music Studio.

  2. 02

    Choose a local folder

    Pick the folder on your machine where finished files should land.

  3. 03

    Start the download

    Paste links or pull your likes. Music Studio downloads and saves what it finishes locally.
