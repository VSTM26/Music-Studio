YouTube Music + Spotify Export Workspace

Music Studio

Launch a guided Chrome session, sign in to either YouTube Music or Spotify, and export your liked songs into shareable .txt, .csv, and .json files. The app uses the live browser session only, so it does not require your own API keys. Optional downloads remain available for YouTube Music exports through yt-dlp and ffmpeg, and you can also paste your own links directly if you already have them.

Workflow

Three clean steps

  1. 01

    Open the guided browser

    We use a dedicated Chrome profile so the export can access a stable debug session for either source.

  2. 02

    Sign in to the selected source

    Sign in with your own account in the special Chrome window, then keep it open while exporting.

  3. 03

    Run the export

    Results land in your selected save folder and are previewed below. If you already have links, use the direct link tools instead of exporting first.

Direct Links

Drop in links you already have

Paste one or more URLs and Music Studio will run them through yt-dlp. This is great for regular YouTube links, playlists, or any other URL that yt-dlp supports.

For best results with YouTube links, sign in with Google to authorize downloads. For videos with special restrictions, you can also open Guided Chrome to reuse that session.
